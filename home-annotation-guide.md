# Home-Page Element Guide

The **card halo** behind each flavor can is an ambient light layer. It gives the product cards depth and makes the subtle 3D tilt effect feel more tactile without adding extra product copy.

The **“Move to tilt” prompt** is a small desktop interaction cue. It tells visitors that the flavor cards respond to pointer movement; on touch devices, the cards remain still for easy reading.

The **product title and description block** is the primary flavor-identification area. It now uses the requested **Berry Bust**, **Straberry**, and **Rose** names, while the new **“Your mood. Your can.”** bar reinforces the campaign idea in the story section.
