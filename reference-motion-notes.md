# Reference Study Notes — Coca-Cola India Home

## Transferable observations

The source site relies on a clean white navigation bar, a generous neutral page background, and a large featured campaign panel that reads like a contained editorial poster rather than a conventional app hero. The page then alternates campaign-style visual banners with quieter information sections, keeping the visual rhythm varied.

The form language most useful to translate is its use of strong field colors, clear image framing, large compact headings, rounded media corners, simple category navigation, and a marquee-like focus on a single featured moment. The original Crimson Spark site will transfer these abstract ideas while using completely distinct copy, product silhouette, imagery, logotype, icons, flavor names, and content architecture.

## Motion translation

The source’s carousel-like featured storytelling becomes one static-first “campaign chamber” with an optional product parallax and carefully staged entrance sequence. Instead of reusing a slider, Crimson Spark will use the impression of a campaign frame, with a custom progress line and product-label plaque.
